About Art by Sana

A small Pakistani studio making art you can live with.

Art by Sana began with one canvas and a love for Urdu script. Today the studio makes paintings, calligraphy and resin art for homes, offices and gifts across Pakistan.

Every order is painted after it is placed, so your piece is never a copy of someone else's. We use artist-grade acrylics, archival varnish and food-safe resin where needed.
